Roadmap

Eight Stages to Bring a Plant Online

01

Project Definition

Confirm product, output, market, quality target, budget boundary, and expansion plan.

02

Site Review

Check land, power, water, discharge, access, climate, and licensing route.

03

Concept Design

Prepare process flow, functional areas, equipment list, utility demand, and layout.

04

Technical Freeze

Define line quantity, sizes, automation, drying concept, and system standards.

05

Engineering & Construction

Provide equipment foundations, loads, clearance, and connection data to local teams.

06

Manufacturing & FAT

Manufacture, inspect, test, pack, and prepare the core equipment for shipment.

07

Installation & Training

Progress from single-machine checks to linked operation, trials, tuning, and training.

08

Validation & Start-up

Support equipment records for client-led validation, licensing, and commercial launch.
